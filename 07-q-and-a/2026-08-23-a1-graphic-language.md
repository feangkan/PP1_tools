# Q&A: Graphic language per colour group (A1)

- **Date:** 2026-08-23
- **Question asked by:** student
- **Resource scope:** `02-assessment-tasks/assessment-1-brief.md`, `00-a1-topics-map.md`, weekly development sheets, tutorial whiteboard, procurement research note

## Question

> Other groups do not have to share Registration’s START / Stage / Gate / Loop logic if they do not have stages. Find a suitable graphic language for each group that can be explained, and that responds to the task.

## Answer

The brief’s first requirement is a **Victoria pathway to registration** with timeframes, gateways, and contingencies. That is the only cluster that *must* read as a linear gated ride. Method stage 2 then names other frameworks to think *with*: pathways, ecosystems, layered systems, networks, cycles, hierarchies. The remaining required topics (education, employment, legislation, bodies, ethics, procurement, research, public value, innovation) must be **related** to that spine with the brief’s verbs — they do not have to be drawn as fake stages.

| Group | Graphic language | Why this language (task + source) |
|---|---|---|
| **REGISTRATION** | Pathway: TRUST/RISK → START → Stages 1–5, fail/re-queue, ejection | Brief item 1. Tutorial whiteboard + Week 3 APE/ARBV. Contingencies: sent back is not ejected; Tribunal is. |
| **EDUCATION** | Formative cycle: train judgment, then keep it alive | Brief: “how is professional judgement developed?” + education incl. CPD. Part 1/2 already sits on gold Stage 1 — do not duplicate it as Education stages. Week 1 integrity; Week 3 Molander/CPD. |
| **EMPLOYMENT** | Project **loop** (F→CA) plus a separate **hours line** | Tutorial whiteboard actually has stages. Career 3,300 hours feed Registration and must stay a different line (tutorial note: once per project vs once per career). Council/BS are legislation checkpoints firing on the loop. |
| **LEGISLATION** | Instrument: write / interpret / amend | Week 5 subject → interpreter → co-author; Act + NCC as provisional law. Conduct clauses and two courts are *uses* of the instrument, not career stages. |
| **ETHICS** | Cycle: knowledge → power → public → knowledge | Week 1 core loop. Answers class Q1–Q2 (profession vs business; duty past the fee-payer). Profit trumping service is not the Tribunal red box. |
| **BODIES** | Split + network | Who maintains standards (Board, statutory) vs who advances the discipline (Institute, voluntary) vs who writes “good” (awards/media). Brief extra prompt: who maintains professional standards. |
| **PROCUREMENT** | Fork of three contracts | Independent research note. Traditional / D&C / novation sit side by side. Critical gap: title intact, authorship gone — a quieter gate than ARBV. |
| **RESEARCH** | Contest: who may rewrite the code | Lui + Week 5 lecture. Bathrooms and carbon are cases, not stages. *Influences* legislation (verb from the brief). How the profession evolves. |
| **POWER** | Overlap (smaller hub) | Brief item 3: power/influence. TheyRule / interlocking seats. Size contrast with Registration *is* the critical position: tight title, loose code and contract. |

Grey arcs between hubs still carry the brief’s verbs: *enables / limits / depends upon / influences / contrasts*. Dotted intra-group marks are only used where that language needs them (fail/re-attempt, project findings, ethics feedback, CPD formative, “new rule answers last”).

**Verbal one-liner:** “Registration is the one compulsory gated pathway. Everything else is related to it with a different graphic language, so you can see where the profession polices tightly and where it does not.”
