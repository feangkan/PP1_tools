## What Sciulli argues (from abstract)

Challenges two common assumptions in sociology of professions:

1. **Professions cannot be distinguished** from other expert / middle-class occupations  
2. **Professionalism’s effects are only socio-economic** (stratification, occupational order) — not broader “social order” or social change

Sciulli responds with two lists:

| Set | What it covers | Challenges |
|-----|----------------|------------|
| **Structural qualities** | Qualities constitutive of professions *uniquely* | Assumption 1 |
| **Institutional consequences** | What professionalism introduces into the larger social order | Assumption 2 |

These are **structural, institutional, and invariant** — they bear on the direction of social change and can be compared cross-nationally and historically.
