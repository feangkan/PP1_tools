## Tutorial workshop (from the Miro board — photos in this folder)

Suggested structure (tutor's note, `miro-workshop-structure.jpg`):

1. **Set up groups + shared workspace** — how you'll work together; shared Miro/Drive/file structure.
2. **Map the project** — key parties and relationships, then significant events chronologically.
3. **Identify change** — where scope, time, cost, quality or responsibilities changed. Ask: *What changed? Who initiated it? Who needed to respond? What were the consequences?*
4. **Investigate possible scenarios** — each student a different event (not limited to the three examples); bring back and pick the one (or connected series) that gives the strongest basis.
5. **Begin the Timeline + Document Register** — working documents that grow as new lectures add material.
6. **Brief report back** — the scenario you're considering, what changed, what an architect would need to communicate or record.

> Recurring question: **"Something changed. What would an architect need to do, communicate and record?"**
>
> Students won't know the answers yet — the lectures on architectural services, contracts and contract administration give the instruments progressively. The three scenarios are starting points; even if you pick one, research the circumstances around the event.

## Key terms

| Term | Definition (in your words) |
|------|----------------------------|
| CAA 2024 | Client Architect Agreement — the contract between client and architect: services, fees, responsibilities, how changes to services are handled |
| ABIC SW 2018 | Australian Building Industry Contract, Simple Works — client ↔ contractor; the architect **administers** it but is not a party |
| Variation | A change to the contracted works, instructed by the architect (SW-12) |
| Adjustment of time | Extension/change to the contract time (SW-14) |
| Contract price adjustment | Change to the contract price (SW-11) |
| Final certificate | The architect's closing certificate of the amount finally payable (SW-31) |
| Document Register | Chronological log of every document issued, by whom, to whom — and who prepared/researched/checked it |
