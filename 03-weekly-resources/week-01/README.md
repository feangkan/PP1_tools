# Week 01 — What is a profession? (Beaton & class discussion)

## Topic

Professionalism in architecture: what distinguishes a profession from a business, who holds power, and where commercial pressure meets professional obligation.

## Learning goals

- Define a profession using Beaton / Cheetham–Chivers characteristics
- Distinguish classic professions, neo-professions, and business
- Identify architects’ obligations beyond the paying client
- Map how Week 1 inquiry feeds **Assessment 1** preparation
