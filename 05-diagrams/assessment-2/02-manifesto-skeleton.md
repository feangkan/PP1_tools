## Overarching position (draft — accept / edit / replace)

> Architecture's authority rests on collective, publicly-accountable mechanisms — registration, the duty to the public, open codes, independent review. These work and must be defended. What is failing is everything that decides **who captures the value of the profession's knowledge and innovation** — privatised standards, opaque rule-making, contracts that strip design authority, and AI whose benefits and risks default to platforms and to the largest practices. The profession must move those decisions back into collective hands.

Grouping option: **Keep the collective (1–5) · Reclaim the decision (6–10)**, or three sections — "Keep collective" (1–4), "Open the knowledge" (5–7), "Reclaim the decision" (8–10).

### Point 8 — Procurement must not be allowed to strip design authority
*"It is vital that a registered architect keeps design control after a project is handed to a contractor."*

| Step | Draft content | Source |
|------|---------------|--------|
| Current condition | Novated design-and-construct (AS4902 + novation) has been standard in Victorian government and commercial work for 20+ years. The AIA's 2019 National Novation Survey (266 practices, 484 projects) found "an enormous opportunity to address building quality and prevent risk." Novation diminishes design intent: lost client contact, restricted site access, exclusion from value management. A registered architect loses control **by contract alone**, without losing registration. | A1 procurement note (`../../02-assessment-tasks/procurement-research-note.md`) |
| Position | **Change.** The Institute's voluntary Code of Novation / Deed of Novation exist but are inadequate as voluntary instruments. | brief §"don't propose what exists unless inadequate" |
| Mechanism | Make a strengthened Code/Deed of Novation a **standard condition** of government procurement; documentation-completeness requirements at the novation point; superintendent independence during construction. | A1 procurement note |
| Actor | State government (as the largest procurer); the Institute; ACA. | — |
| Action | Victorian government mandates the Deed of Novation on public projects; the Institute lobbies for it in standard forms. | — |
| Consequence | The architect legally responsible for the design keeps the authority to protect it; building quality improves (the Institute's own evidence). **Tension:** contractors' and clients' resistance; cost/programme claims. This is the operational counterpart to Point 2's legal duty. | A1 procurement note |
