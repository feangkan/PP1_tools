## 1. The one structural move

Assessment 1 answered **"how does the profession operate, and where is power mismatched?"** — its critical position was: *the profession polices its one visible gate (registration) far more tightly than its less visible ones (who writes the code, who keeps design control after novation).*

Assessment 2 asks you to **turn that map into a position**: for each mismatch or working part, say **maintain / refine / change**, name the **actor** and **instrument**, and state the **consequence**. The A1 diagram already contains the raw clusters; A2 re-labels each cluster's relationships as a **judgement + a proposed action**.

So the re-engineering is literally: **A1 cluster → A2 manifesto point**, sorted into the two halves.

## 3. Which topics the manifesto should pursue (recommended 5 + 5)

Chosen for: (a) each has real A1 or Week 6/7 evidence already in the repo, (b) together they make **one** argument — *the profession's collective, publicly-accountable mechanisms work; the decision over who captures the value of the profession's knowledge, innovation and labour is being privatised and individualised, and that is where reform should go.*

### MAINTAIN / REFINE (5)

1. **Registration as a trust threshold, with real supervision** — maintain the APE (esp. the interview) and PI insurance; refine the logbook so supervision requires real capacity, not just a signature. *(A1 Registration Pathway)*
2. **Duty to the public over the paying client** — maintain Architects Act s.17–18 and the duty to withdraw; extends to the people producing the building, not only those occupying it. *(A1 Ethics & Public Value; Week 7)*
3. **Third-party sign-off per project** — maintain independent Building Surveyor / Council checkpoints as a working public-protection gate. *(A1 Project Delivery Process)*
4. **The split apparatus + the bodies that turn experience into evidence** — maintain the Institute/Board split; refine by funding UAW/Parlour/the wellbeing research. *(A1 Professional Bodies; Week 7)*
5. **Open, amendable codes and standards (NCC/AS) + CPD as formative judgement and commercial literacy** — maintain the codes as an open instrument; refine CPD to include how the business of architecture actually works. *(A1 Research & Innovation + CPD; Week 7)*

### CHANGE (5)

6. **Stop the privatisation of professional knowledge** — reverse/limit the Standards Australia → SAI Global pattern; keep code-feeding research open. *(Week 6 §F + A1 Research & Innovation)*
7. **Rule-making transparency** — who amends codes/standards, and the overlapping leadership across schools/Institute/Board, should be visible and contestable. *(A1 Power Structure / "TheyRule")*
8. **Procurement must not separate design responsibility from design authority** — the flagship case: a strengthened Deed of Novation, standard in government projects. *(A1 Procurement)*
9. **Working conditions need a collective instrument** — individual resilience/negotiation/escape is not a mechanism for a structural problem; recognise a worker body, benchmark fees, clarify the award. *(Week 7 — replaces v01's AI-accountability point)*
10. **Claim authority over where the value of the work goes** — fee/resourcing transparency in public procurement; a norm on where efficiency gains (AI included, as one example) land. *(Week 6 §D + Week 7 fee economics — replaces v01's AI-value-capture point)*

**Overarching position (draft — the student must own this):**
> *Architecture's collective, publicly-accountable mechanisms — registration, the duty to the public, open codes, independent review — work and must be defended. What is failing is who gets to decide the value of the profession's knowledge, its innovation, and its labour: that decision is being captured privately or individually instead of staying with the profession collectively. This shows sharpest in procurement and in working conditions, where the person carrying responsibility for an outcome no longer holds authority over it.*

## 4. What NOT to reuse verbatim

- The A1 **150-word critical position** is written to a different brief (a reading note for a diagram). Its *argument* (visible vs invisible gates) is the manifesto's spine; its wording is not.
- The A1 **theme-park metaphor** was already dropped at v07 — don't revive it.
- A1's **procurement note** is independent research tagged for A1; re-cite it for A2, don't present it as unit content.
- Don't try to fit all ten A1 required topics in — the brief says "use the concepts that help you make and support your argument."
