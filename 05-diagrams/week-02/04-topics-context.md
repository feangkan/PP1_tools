## Extraction method note

`Week 2_Weekly task.pdf` has a non-standard font encoding that produces garbled text with a direct PDF text extractor (`pdfplumber`). It was re-read via page rasterisation + OCR (`tesseract`) to recover accurate text; this revealed it is an Outlook email export ("PP1 — Welcome to Week 3!", from Jack Stirling, dated the Friday before Week 3) rather than a standalone task brief. Content above is taken from the OCR pass, cross-checked visually against the rendered page images.

## Sciulli structural-quality count — flagged for verification

The source paper states it isolates **eight** structural qualities (p. 12), but the bulleted list in the text can be read as seven or eight distinct bullets depending on how the two fiducial-responsibility sub-points are counted. This diagram groups them into a single "structured situations, authority, fiducial duty, merit, jurisdiction" node for space — **re-check the exact count and wording against pp. 12–15 before quoting a specific number in Assessment 1.**
