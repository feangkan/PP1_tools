## Hook (15 sec)

“This week treats the NCC as an instrument of law. It enables buildings to exist. It is not a neutral manual, and it is provisional — written, interpreted, amended, repealed.”

## Walk the diagram (60–90 sec)

“Three roles. Subject: what must I do. Interpreter: why does this requirement exist and what values does it hold. Co-author: who gets to change it.

The NCC cluster is measurable translation: class and storeys set construction type; heights, light and air become numbers.

The dotted loop is who gets written in: lived experience, advocacy, consultation, then a code change. The slide path for all-gender bathrooms is 2023 proposal, 2024 consultation, NCC 2025, Deemed-to-Satisfy in May 2026.

1666 sits beside it because the lecture uses the same four tags: types, construction, minimum dimensions, measurable light and air — plus time, neighbours, disputes, property.”

## Critical takeaway (20–30 sec)

“Lui’s line on the slide: architects can move from subjects of regulation to participants in authorship. That is the A1 RESEARCH cluster: the profession polices the title more tightly than who writes the code.”

## Close (10 sec)

“Happy to take questions on subject versus co-author, or on how this sits on the gold registration path.”
