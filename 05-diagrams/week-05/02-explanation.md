## One-sentence overview

> This diagram shows the Building Code / NCC as a **provisional instrument of law** that **enables** buildings to exist: the architect is first a **subject** of the rule, then an **interpreter** of its intention, then a **co-author** when advocacy amends the code.

## How to read the diagram (order)

1. Start at the three roles: Subject → Interpreter → Co-author.
2. Read the NCC cluster: class, type, height, light, air — measurable translations, not taste.
3. Follow “law is provisional”: written → interpreted → amended → repealed.
4. Trace who gets written in: lived experience → advocacy → consultation → NCC change → future buildings.
5. Glance at 1666: the same four tags as today’s NCC (types, construction, dimensions, light/air).
6. End at the tensions: bathrooms / livable housing / moisture paradox; Hill and Fire/Water/Building stay tagged as pre-reading or title-only.

## Cluster-by-cluster

### Roles — subject / interpreter / co-author

- **What it means:** Closing slides: instruments enable buildings; they are not neutral; they are provisional, not optional. Questions: what must I do? why does this requirement exist? who gets to change it?
- **Why it is on the diagram:** It is the week’s organising claim and the A1 RESEARCH / LEGISLATION graphic language (instrument + contest).
- **Link:** Feeds NCC (what the subject complies with) and the dotted amendment loop (how a co-author acts).

### NCC — measurable translations

- **What it means:** A6 classifications + rise in storeys set Type A/B/C; C2D2 type determines construction; F5D2 heights; F6 light and ventilation converted into percentages and openable area.
- **Why it is on the diagram:** The lecture’s “today” content — the code as measurable translation of values.
- **Link:** **Enables** the subject role; 1666 uses the same four tags.

### Provisional law + who gets written in

- **What it means:** Written → interpreted → amended → repealed. Lived path: 2023 proposal → 2024 consultation → NCC 2025; all-gender bathrooms adopted as a Deemed-to-Satisfy option (May 2026 on the slide). Lui: architects can move from subjects of regulation to participants in authorship.
- **Why it is on the diagram:** Answers “how does the profession continue to evolve?”
- **Link:** Dotted feedback from future buildings back to lived experience.

### Cases and tensions

- **What it means:** Livable housing: yesterday’s exemplar is today’s minimum. Energy-efficient tight envelopes can trap moisture — a new rule answers the last rule. Cooke (1991): the “good” is assumed written into scheme and code vs the will of the people. Hill carbon cascade and Fire/Water/Building are **pre-reading / title-slide only** — do not present them as lecture cases unless you heard them in class.
- **Why it is on the diagram:** These are the week’s named tensions, flagged honestly.
- **Link:** Moisture and bathrooms sit on RESEARCH in the A1 stigmergy sheet.

## Critical point (your position)

The gold A1 path polices **who may be called Architect**. Week 5 shows a quieter gate: **who may rewrite the baseline rules**. That is the same critical position as Lui + POWER on the stigmergy sheet.

## If someone asks “so what?”

Registration is a gated career ride. The code is an instrument that can be rewritten. If the profession only polishes the title and never contests the instrument, public value (bathrooms, carbon, moisture, livable housing) stays downstream of someone else’s authorship. Point at gold, then at RESEARCH → LEGISLATION *influences*.
