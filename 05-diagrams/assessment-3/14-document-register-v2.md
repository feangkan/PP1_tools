## Two new rows explained (both Lead 1's to draft)

- **Client representative change**: CAA clause C.3 — either party may replace its representative, with notice to the other. Cahill to Askin is exactly this kind of event, and it isn't in the register yet even though it's a real change of who the "Client" is throughout the rest of the file.
- **Change to architectural services**: CAA clause D.8 — when a project needs a greater scope of services, the architect must promptly notify the client how that affects the fee, then agree it in writing. The SW-12 variation (Prani Patton's row) covers the *construction* side of the piers redesign; this new row is its *design-services* twin, and without it the file only tells half the story of who had to do what.
