## Decide as a group

1. Dates for the ★ `A` rows: one date per form, fixed once, then copied to the Register and the forms.
2. **Waagner-Biro: under the builder, or client-direct?** If client-direct, the omission is a separate contract/owner-supplied item, not a J1 variation to Hornibrook's contract.
3. **Amounts** for SW-11 / SW-31: find the real figures (Murray 2004; Hansard 1967), or declare `[A]`.
4. The change of architect (28 Feb / 19 Apr 1966) must show on both timelines, because the forms after 1966 are issued by Hall's team.
