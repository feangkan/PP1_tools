**Recommended: A (Major Hall change of use).** It's the clearest **client-initiated design change** an architect has to manage. It needs the architect to advise, redesign, instruct, price and record, and it touches every factor: scope, time, cost and quality (acoustics, sightlines, seat numbers). One cost: the file needs Utzon's letter + CAA, then a **recorded change of architect** (Utzon resigns Feb 1966 → Hall, Todd and Littlemore) before the forms. That also gives Part B its strongest material.

**If the group wants Utzon throughout: B (forced early start).** Pure architect-client advice, with contract consequences.
