Develop the relevant **SWC-2018 forms**: Variation to the Scope of Work, Adjustment of Time, Contract Price Adjustment, Final Certificate. ## Assessment criteria (Method + Assessment Criteria tab, in the tutor's own words)

- Ability to understand, reference and synthesise information and procedures established through **AIA Acumen practice notes**
- Detail, understanding and clarity of the brief and clauses of the **Client Architect Agreement** that clearly communicate terms, services to be provided, and fees appropriate for the scale and type of project
- Ability to understand the key roles an architect performs in a contract, and a complex range of architectural issues and situations including **legal and ethical obligations relating to copyright and intellectual property**
- Ability to analyse, present and argue the contract components in a clear, cohesive and structured manner, including demonstrating communication with the client and relevant stakeholders throughout the project
- Use of relevant academic references and bibliographic support material; appropriate footnoting and bibliography

### AACA National Standard of Competency for Architects — performance criteria this course addresses

| Code | Criterion |
|------|-----------|
| PC5 | Understand the essential elements of a client architect agreement, across the range of procurement methods and the different scales and types of project |
| PC6 | Understand appropriate processes for reporting and varying the scope of services provided by an architect |
| PC7 | Understand appropriate processes for clear and consistent communication with clients and relevant stakeholders throughout a project, including obtaining approvals from clients and stakeholders |
| PC15 | Understand legal and ethical obligations relating to copyright, moral rights, authorship of cultural knowledge and intellectual property requirements across architectural services |
| PC54 | Understand the purpose of periodic site visits of construction works for quality assurance |
| PC56 | Understand the purpose of identification of defects, rectifications and approval substitutions |
| PC57 | Understand the principles of contract administration, including certification, variations, instructions, requests for information and practical completion |
| PC58 | Understand the contract components — including all documents — and the process of executing a contract, as defined within the construction contract and in accordance with relevant building and planning codes |

**Use these codes directly in Part B** — mapping specific project decisions to specific PC codes (e.g. "assessing the contractor's claim under H4 demonstrates PC57") is exactly the kind of "specific decisions and documents as evidence" the brief and rubric Criteria 3 ask for.
