## D1 — Client + brief

**13 Sep 1955 — Competition announced by Premier Cahill (two halls)**  
Premier Cahill announces an international design competition for an opera house at Bennelong Point: two halls (3,000–3,500 and 1,200 seats), a restaurant and meeting rooms. This is the original client brief.  
*Sources:* [D: Museums of History NSW — Sydney Opera House guide (chronology)](https://mhnsw.au/guides/sydney-opera-house/)

**Feb 1957 — Introductory letter** · **GL1**  `REG [A]`  
Our first required document (GL1): the architect introduces the practice and the services to the client. The Register has no date; Feb 1957 (after the win) is our assumption.  
*Sources:* REG: The group's Document Register (Week 10 / DOCUMENT REGISTER_PP1.pdf)

**21 Feb 1957 — CAA signed — must be the 2024 edition (Register says 2019)** · **CAA**  
The Client Architect Agreement is signed (Register: 21 Feb 1957). Our file must use the CAA 2024 template — the Register currently says CAA 2019.  
*Sources:* REG: The group's Document Register (Week 10 / DOCUMENT REGISTER_PP1.pdf)

**22 Oct 1959 — Premier Cahill dies; Heffron Premier — client rep changes (CAA C.3)**  
Cahill dies in office on 22 Oct 1959 (ADB; the archive guide says 19 Oct) and Heffron becomes Premier. The client's representative changes, which CAA C.3 requires the client to notify.  
*Sources:* [U: Australian Dictionary of Biography — Cahill, John Joseph (Joe)](https://adb.anu.edu.au/biography/cahill-john-joseph-joe-9659) · [W: Wikipedia — Robert Askin (premier dates; also Bob Heffron, Jack Renshaw, Tom Lewis pages)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Askin) · [D: Museums of History NSW — Sydney Opera House guide (chronology)](https://mhnsw.au/guides/sydney-opera-house/)

**Jan 1960 — Sydney Opera House Act 1960: Minister for Public Works = constructing authority**  
The 1960 Act makes the Minister for Public Works the constructing authority (backdated to 1 Jan 1957) and sets up the lottery account. Legally, the client who signs the building contracts is the Minister.  
*Sources:* [D: Museums of History NSW — Sydney Opera House guide (chronology)](https://mhnsw.au/guides/sydney-opera-house/) · [Z: NSW Treasury — The House the Lotteries Built](https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/nsw-treasury/about-us/nsw-treasury-bicentenary/walking-a-tightrope/moments/house-lotteries-built)

**14 Mar 1961 — Trust Act 1961: Sydney Opera House Trust set up**  
The Sydney Opera House Trust Act (assented 14 Mar 1961) creates the Trust that will run the building, replacing the Executive Committee. The Trust operates the House; it did not build it.  
*Sources:* [V: NSW Legislation — Sydney Opera House Trust Act 1961 No 9](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/act-1961-009)
