## Open gaps for v02

- **Victorian statutory registration year (1922 vs 1923):** the recording states "enacted the Act... in nineteen twenty two" earlier, then "the first statutory registration only occurred in 1923" a few lines later, then reverts to "1922" when discussing the first 33 registrants. This looks like an auto-transcription/speech artefact rather than two different facts — cross-check the exact year against the Architects Act 1991's own historical notes or the ARBV website before quoting a single year in Assessment 1.
- **APE handbook page references** ("pages 17 and 18" for verification/signature requirements) mentioned in the recording but the handbook itself is not in this week's folder — flag as a resource to add if the APE section is developed further.
- **Molander et al. subclass mapping** (formative/supportive/motivational/deliberative/participatory applied one-to-one to every ARBV mechanism) is only sketched at cluster level on this diagram — a genuinely rich v02 companion diagram could work through this mapping exhaustively (see note in `01-diagram.md` redraw notes).
- **Slide deck 1 is 800+ text-lines** long once extracted (includes an embedded, lengthy LinkedIn comment thread on the NSW repeal post) — only the substantive Act/history content was used above; the comment-thread names/quotes were treated as incidental screenshot content, not cited as sources.
