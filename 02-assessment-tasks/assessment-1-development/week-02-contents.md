## Six frameworks (pick lenses; do not copy all six equally onto A1)

### 1. Traits model — evidence method

Place the **registered architect** in the centre. Five traits around it; find evidence for each:

| Trait | Evidence pointer |
|---|---|
| Disciplinary knowledge | Acumen, journals, canon |
| Regulation | ARBV, Architects Act, NCC |
| Autonomy | Judgment, signing, sign-off |
| Altruism | Code of conduct, client not in the room |
| Higher learning | AQF master’s-level inquiry |

Priority order is contested (university vs practice vs AIA).

### 2. Continuum — Goode and Moore

Occupation ←→ profession.

- Knowledge base + service ideal + public trust  
- Skilled trade = **repeatable competence** (potter)  
- Profession = **judgment on a novel subject**  
- Nurse ↔ doctor boundary is shifting  
- Builder ↔ architect boundary overlaps  

### 3. Apparatus — institutions, laws, power

- Bodies: ARBV, AACA, AIA, university, unions  
- Instruments: Acts, Regulations, Codes, Contracts  
- Live poll: who influences the final building — client, builder, architect, regulator, finance?  
- Influence may sit **in relationships**, not one actor  
- Bodies renew every 5–6 years — power is not fixed  

*(Recording sometimes says “WACA” — treat as ARBV unless you check the slide.)*

### 4. Kuhn — Structure of Scientific Revolutions (RED)

Normal practice → anomalies accumulate → crisis (cladding, cracked slabs) → revolution (contested) → new paradigm ···→ normal.

Class poll: most said **crisis**, few said revolution. Those holding institutional power often have least incentive to shift.

### 5. Plato’s Divided Line

```
ABSTRACT:  laws, codes, disciplinary knowledge, IP
ON THE LINE:  drawings + contract  (instruments)
PHYSICAL:  building, materials, client, builder
THEN:  exemplar → award / journal → feeds back into knowledge
```

The building does not take material form until it is **administered through the contract**.

### 6. Sciulli — structural + institutional invariance (GREEN)

Challenges: (1) professions cannot be distinguished from other expert occupations; (2) effects are only socio-economic.

| Set | Short labels (grouped — confirm count before quoting “8”) |
|---|---|
| Structural qualities | Structured situations · authority · fiducial duty · merit · jurisdiction *(paper: eight; grouping ambiguous)* |
| Institutional consequences | Authority + governance · upgraded discourse · restrained power · supports democratic design |

**Case:** Paris visual Académie (1648–80) — meritocratic anonymous student competitions; professionalised two centuries before English law; aristocrats once designed their own houses (pre-professional backdrop).

## Critical point (for A1)

The registration spine is legitimate if it is a **structured, merit-gated, judgment-bearing** pathway (Sciulli + continuum), sitting inside a **contested apparatus**, translating abstract knowledge through **drawings and contracts**. If you only draw a checklist, you miss why the spine exists.
