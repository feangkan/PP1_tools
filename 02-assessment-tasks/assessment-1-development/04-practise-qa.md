## Gold path

### 1. Why stigmergy, not a flowchart?

> The sheet is one field where colour groups leave marks that later actors read. A flowchart would invent a single owner of the whole matrix. Stigmergy keeps co-production visible.

**Source:** `02-explanation.md`; catalog graphic-language table.

### 2. Walk the gold path in under 30 seconds.

> TRUST and RISK before START. Stage 1: accredited education, AQF Part 1 and 2, gated. Stage 2: logbook, 3,300 hours, 35 competencies, ongoing. Stage 3: APE — 80 MCQ, interview 45 to 60 minutes, gated. Fail re-queues on the dotted loop — sent back, not ejected. Stage 4: ARBV grants the title. Stage 5: CPD and PI loop. Tribunal ejection is a different path after serious breach.

**Source:** catalog R1–R12; `tutorial-whiteboard.md`.

### 3. Fail versus ejected — what is the difference?

> Fail or incomplete hours send the candidate back to re-attempt that stage. Ejection is a Tribunal finding after serious breach — loss of title, not a dotted re-queue on the exam.

**Source:** catalog R7, R11, R12.

### 4. Why is gold larger than the other hubs?

> Size marks hierarchy of **force**: registration is the tight statutory path. It does not mean gold is “more important as a person” or that other topics are decoration.

**Source:** `02-explanation.md`; script “Do not say.”

## Other languages

### 5. Why don’t Education or Employment use Stage 1, 2, 3?

> Those stage words belong to registration. Education is a formative cycle; Employment is a project loop plus a separate hours line. Copying gold language would fake a second ARBV track.

**Source:** catalog graphic-language table; Part B of the script.

### 6. Where do Part 1 and Part 2 sit?

> On gold Stage 1. The green Education hub is about how judgment is trained and kept alive (studio, integrity, CPD) — not a duplicate ladder.

**Source:** catalog EDUCATION zoom-out; `02-explanation.md`.

### 7. How does Employment relate to Stage 2?

> The project loop runs once per job: feasibility through contract administration, with Council and Building Surveyor as external gates. The hours line is separate: 3,300 logged hours and 35 competencies feed Stage 2. Phases are not the same as hours.

**Source:** `tutorial-whiteboard.md`; catalog EMPLOYMENT.

### 8. What is the Bodies split?

> The Institute is voluntary — advocacy, awards, CPDs. The ARBV is statutory — register and prosecute. Same field, different force. Awards can manufacture “good” without granting the title.

**Source:** catalog BODIES; week packs in this folder.

### 9. What is Procurement doing on the sheet?

> A fork of three contracts: traditional, novated design and construct, managing contractor. A registered architect can lose design control by contract alone. That fork is **independent research**, not from the set four readings.

**Source:** catalog PROCUREMENT; `how-to-read-and-critical-position.md` notes; bibliography AIA procurement page.

## Critical position and sources

### 10. What is the critical claim in one sentence?

> The profession polices who may be called Architect more tightly than who writes the code or who keeps authorship after novation.

**Source:** catalog critical-position line; 150-word note; title question on the sheet.

### 11. Beaton / Loos / Lui / AIA — one beat each?

| Name | Speak this | Do not invent |
|---|---|---|
| Beaton | Title as **institution** — who is trusted to hold it | — |
| Loos | Ornament / taste / class inside “professional” appearance | Collection or page number |
| Lui | A code of conduct can be **rewritten**; rewrite = power | Year or page range |
| AIA *Code of Conduct* | **Voluntary** professional text, not the Act | That it is statute |

**Source:** `06-references/a1-stigmergy-bibliography.md` (missing fields flagged).

### 12. Where do TRUST and RISK come from?

> Tutorial whiteboard (class notes), not a publication. Autonomy, discretion, accountability under TRUST; risk owed to public and profession. Do not invent a citation for that session.

**Source:** `tutorial-whiteboard.md`.

### 13. Who holds power — and is that where it should sit? (Week 1 class prompt)

> On the sheet, power is the smaller overlapping hub across school, Institute, and Board. The claim is that the tight gate sits on the title, while code-writing and contracts stay looser — so the open question is whether that distribution serves the public, not only the fee-payer.

**Source:** brief Week 1 Q3; catalog POWER / X10–X12; Beaton framing in the note.

### 14. How does the profession evolve?

> Legislation and research are drawn as rewriteable instruments and a contest over who may amend them. Stage 5 CPD keeps judgment alive after the title is granted. Evolution is not only more gates on gold.

**Source:** catalog LEGISLATION / RESEARCH; brief “Extra critical prompts.”
