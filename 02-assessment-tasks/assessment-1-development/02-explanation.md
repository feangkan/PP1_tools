# Explanation — A1 stigmergy (verbal, Week 5)

Use this with the Rhino sheet (V17 or later). Spoken version: `03-presentation-script.md`.

## What it is

This is a **stigmergy** diagram of Victoria’s architecture **disciplinary matrix**. It is not an organisational chart and not a timeline. Colour groups sit in the same field and leave marks that later actors read. The claim is that registration is **tight** (one gold path, gated, fail-and-retry) while the **code of conduct** and **procurement contracts** stay **looser** — and that looseness is where trust, risk, and power sit.

## How to read the sheet

1. **Start at gold REGISTRATION** (largest hub, lower-centre). Follow **TRUST → RISK → START**, then Stages 1–5. Dotted loops are **fail / re-queue / re-attempt**. Tribunal **ejection** is a different path — not the same as failing the APE.
2. **Do not copy gold onto other colours.** Each group has its **own graphic language** (cycle, fork, instrument, contest). See the table below.
3. **Grey verbs** (enables / limits / depends upon / influences / contrasts) are the only permitted links **between** groups. They are relations, not stages.
4. **Title** in the upper void: *DISCIPLINARY MATRIX* / *Trusted with the title — by whom, for what?* Read it as the question the gold path never fully answers.
5. **POWER** is the smaller overlapping hub — not a second registration track.

## Gold path (keep this order)

TRUST → RISK → START → Stage 1 Accredited education AQF Part 1/2 (**gated**) → Stage 2 Supervised logbook 3,300 hrs / 35 competencies (**ongoing**) → Stage 3 APE 80 MCQ + interview 45–60 min (**gated**) → Fail / re-queue (sent back, not ejected) → Re-attempt → Stage 4 ARBV granted (**gated**) → Stage 5 CPD + PI loop → serious breach → **Ejected** (Tribunal) → back toward Stage 1.

## If someone asks “so what?”

The profession polices **who may hold the title** more tightly than **who writes the code** or **who keeps authorship after novation**. Gold is the tight gate. Conduct and contract are the loose marks. The title on the sheet is the unanswered question.
