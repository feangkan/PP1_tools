## One-sentence

The Act gives **legal form** to knowledge, authority and responsibility. The APE is the entry gate; compliance culture argues the gate is not enough — judgment must stay accountable after registration.

## Cluster B — Institute vs Board (network)

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| AIA | Voluntary — architects act together; advances discipline |
| ARBV | Statutory — society holds architects accountable; minimum standard |
| Contrast | Medicine’s College of GPs collapses both into one |

**Specialist network (fills AIA gaps):**

- ArchiTeam — small-practice collective, shared insurance  
- Architects Declare Australia — climate / biodiversity  
- AASA — schools / pre-reg knowledge  
- Parlour — gender equity; **21% registered women vs ~40% of graduates**  
- ACA — business, **procurement**, employment conditions  
- AACA — NSCA; coordinates state/territory boards  

**International:** national/state regulation (AU, Canada, Germany, US/NCARB) vs voluntary (Denmark, Sweden, Finland). India mirrors the split (Council + Institute). Peggy Deamer: US licensure regulates **entry**, not wages/hours/insecure work.

**Persistent Australian problem (lecture):** not too few institutions — they are **fragmented**.

## Cluster D — Registration spine (THIS is the A1 mechanism)

**5 requirements:** fit & proper (probity — criminal history, insolvency — **not** physical fitness; disclose upfront) · prescribed study · **2 years** practice · PII · fees  

**2 classes:** practising · non-practising  

**3 pathways:** APE · Experienced Practitioner Assessment (portfolio; hard; overseas / long experience) · Mutual Recognition (interstate/overseas; bypass re-proving experience)

### APE (most students)

| Part | What to draw |
|---|---|
| **1 Logbook + SOPE** | 3,300 hrs · 35 performance criteria · SOPE 2,000–3,000 words · ~4 projects typical · CV/PC report excluded from count |
| Hour caps | Max 1,650 hrs pre-graduation; same cap overseas; **min 12 months Australian post-grad** |
| PC categories (4) | Practice Mgmt & Professional Conduct · Project Initiation & Conceptual Design · Detailed Design & Construction Documentation · Design Delivery & Construction Phase Services |
| **2 National Exam** | 120 min · 80 MCQ · closed book · NCC-focused |
| **3 Interview** | 45–60 min · probes **gaps** via hypotheticals · tip: breadth, not “coolest” project |
| After pass | **Still must apply** for registration |

**Contingencies for the A1 spine:** fail/repeat exam · incomplete hours · interstate/overseas · Tribunal / discipline register (exclusion)

## Cluster F — Molander et al. 2012 (GREEN theory)

Discretion = structural **space** to judge + epistemic **reasoning** under uncertainty.

Tensions: discretion vs rule of law · discretion vs democratic control (“democracy’s black hole”).

Accountability = duty to justify to those with a right to demand it.

| Measure type | A1 mapping |
|---|---|
| Structural | Constrain space — registration, Tribunal |
| Formative | Education, CPD |
| Supportive | Evidence-based practice, decision-support |
| Motivational | Incentives / nudge — risk of “gaming” |
| Deliberative (narrow) | Tribunal, interview |
| Deliberative (wide) | Public sphere, public discipline register |
| Participatory | Co-decision with affected parties |

Norwegian GP case: 360 GPs, wide variation in disability-pension judgments, correlated with personal values.

## Critical point (for A1)

The Act’s legitimacy is whether its **mechanisms** (registration, Tribunal, CPD, public register) make discretion justifiable. Compliance culture is a bet that the system over-invested in punishing breaches and under-invested in building judgment **before** it fails.
