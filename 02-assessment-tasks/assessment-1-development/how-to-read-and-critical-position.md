ARBV grants the title; CPD and PI then loop. Only a Tribunal finding ejects the title.

Around that spine, education keeps judgment alive while employment loops a project beside those hours, and legislation is written, interpreted, and amended. Ethics cycles knowledge, power, and public value. Bodies split Institute from Board. Procurement forks three contracts; research contests who rewrites the code; power sits smaller.

Grey lines enable, limit, depend upon, influence, and contrast. The profession polices who may be called Architect more tightly than who writes the code or who keeps authorship after novation, and energy should move there (Beaton 2010; Loos 1910; Lui; AIA 2019).
