## One-sentence

Loos (1910): there are no good or bad architects — only degrees of the same guilt. Awards, journals and social media **manufacture difference**. Closing test: does a mechanism serve the public, or only make one architect more marketable?

## Cluster B — Awards as a value system

AIA categories (short list): Commercial · Educational · Heritage · Interior · Public · Residential (New/Alts/Multiple) · Small Project · Urban Design · Sustainable · Colorbond Steel · Enduring · Emerging · EmAGN · Regional · Melbourne · Bates Smart Media

- Are these different **Architectures**, or different **business models / marketing**?  
- Awards = “professional constitution of value” — decide in advance what counts: typology, authorship, visual distinction, technical skill  
- What stays peripheral: social benefit, working on country, collaboration, maintenance, long-term occupation  

**Case — *Judging Architecture* 1929–2003:** 1969 Southland Shopping Centre beat the NGV (no categories then — merit + contribution). Retrospect reversed the verdict (suburban retail R&D vs cultural value).

**Case:** Melbourne Park Tennis Centre (NH Architecture) — TV studio / sport / concert — which single category?

**Freeland:** Vic journal (RVIA 1929–42) = professional record; NSW *Art + Architecture* (1908) = public culture. Journals/awards = machinery that decides **who represents** the profession.

## Cluster E — Media ecosystem (six channels)

| Channel | Rewards | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Awards | Recognised excellence | Prestige among those who can enter + document |
| Journals | Selected projects, argument | Criticism and promotion hard to separate |
| Online design media | Novelty, global reach | Detached from local context (e.g. ArchDaily) |
| Practice site + PR | Brand narrative | No independent evaluation |
| Instagram / TikTok | Images, frequency, engagement | Popularity = value proxy |
| LinkedIn | Leadership persona | Authority via repetition, not merit |

Independence spectrum: owners, advertisers, sponsors, architects who grant access + photography, shared institutional networks.

**Bates Smart Award for Architecture in Media** (est. 1986) — three-year swing:

- 2023: Australian Indigenous Design Charter + “Deadly Djurumin Yarns” (Parlour) — redistributes authority  
- 2025: March Studio monograph (Fleur Watson) — “strategic point in career” — consolidates individual status  
- 2026: Izzie White, TikTok/Instagram ~50,000 — public literacy, personal brand, or both?

**Case — A’Beckett Tower 2011** (Elenberg Fraser): Beyoncé-body façade won Best Overend Residential; pre–Better Apartment Design Standards; bedrooms with no windows. Debate was the façade, not the windows. $500 dining-voucher Instagram competition ran alongside.

## Cluster H — Cameron Bruhn extracts

- Media as **tripart loop**: public event → magazine → award → back into content  
- Start from **public value**, not the individual (housing crisis / Anglicare data)  
- Post-GFC diversification freed magazines from news reporting  
- Prefab dreamed since Crystal Palace 1851, re-dreamed every decade, never delivered; construction near crisis; not equipped for 2050 net-zero  
- Profession **ceded project management** in the 1960s–70s — now a lucrative separate field  
- Multiple roles proposed: agency beyond design (Bruhn) · government/policy · cultural producer (Stead) · not over-promoting individuals (Shaw) · trust granted by society (Bailey)

**Lecture list of what the profession risks ignoring while it “celebrates itself”:** housing · climate · country · labour conditions · **procurement** · public trust
