## Entrance framing for the registration spine

### TRUST (red oval on the board)

Autonomy · Discretion · Accountability  

Every gate on the pathway tests one of these three — not “knowledge” alone.

### RISK (blue oval on the board)

Owed to the **PUBLIC** and the **PROFESSION**.

Board equation read roughly as **knowledge = risk**, plus experience. Marks on the board look revised or emphasised.

**Use this reading, not a fake formula:** more knowledge + experience → more risk you are trusted to carry, owed to both public and profession.

**Medium confidence** on the exact equation. Confirm wording with your tutor before quoting it in the 150-word note.

## Project delivery process (green — nest inside Employment)

This loop runs **once per project**. The registration spine runs **once per career**. Do not draw them as the same line.

```
CLIENT + STAKEHOLDERS → BRIEF
        ↓
Feasibility (F) → Sketch / Concept (SK) → Developed Design (DD)
        → Construction Documentation (CD) → Contract Administration (CA)
```

**NSCA {Competencies}** bracket spans **both** the design process **and** Contract Administration — not design skill only.

### External gates (downward arrows on the board)

| Gate | When it bites |
|---|---|
| Town Planning (Council) | From the design process (often around sketch / developed) |
| Building Surveyor (BS) | From documentation / construction |

### Feedback

A long dashed arrow curved back along the bottom. Direction/endpoint inferred.

**Use this reading:** the ride is a **loop**, not a straight line — Council/BS findings return to client + brief.
