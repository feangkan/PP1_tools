| Local | `03-weekly-resources/week-05/(Prereading) Building Code as Battleground- Activism, Amendments, and (Co)Authorship - Harvard Design Magazine.url` |

**Draw-ready claim:** codes are a site of **co-authorship**. Amendments come through activism, not expertise alone.

**Four historical examples used on the main A1 (verify against the article before quoting):**

| Year / period | Amendment story |
|---|---|
| 1911 | Triangle Shirtwaist Fire → egress / fire requirements |
| 1958–73 | Removal of explicitly segregationist plumbing codes |
| 1970s–90s | Disability rights → access requirements (504 Sit-In, ADA) |
| 2018 | Gender-inclusive bathroom code amendments |

**A1 use:** Legislation is not a fixed backstage machine. Research & Innovation is where that becomes visible. Contrast with Week 3’s tightly gated APE.

- 200,000 homes/year at current practice ≈ **200%** of Australia’s emissions budget  
- Proposed fix is **regulatory**: cascading embodied-carbon limits in the **National Construction Code**  
- Current average **461.8** → **6.63** kgCO₂e/m²·a by **2028**

**A1 use:** Innovation attached to **legislation** (NCC), not only to new geometry or products.
