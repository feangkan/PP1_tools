## 60-second version (Part A) — learn this first

*[Point at title, then gold hub.]*

This sheet is Victoria’s architecture **disciplinary matrix**, drawn as **stigmergy** — colour groups leaving marks in one field.

**Read gold first.** **TRUST** and **RISK** sit before **START**. **Stage 1:** accredited education, AQF Part 1 and 2 — **gated**. **Stage 2:** supervised logbook — **3,300 hours**, **35 competencies** — **ongoing**. **Stage 3:** the APE — **80** multiple choice, interview **45 to 60 minutes** — **gated**. **Fail** is the dotted loop: sent back, **not ejected**. **Stage 4:** ARBV grants the title. **Stage 5:** CPD and PI loop. **Ejection** is different — Tribunal, serious breach.

Other colours **do not copy** this ladder. Grey verbs — enables, limits, depends upon — link the groups.

The title asks: **trusted with the title — by whom, for what?** Registration is **tight**. Conduct and procurement stay **looser**. That is where trust and power sit.

## ~3-minute version (Part B) — after Part A

**Other languages (pick two if short on time; all five if they let you run):**

**Education** is a **formative cycle** — studio, exam, next year. Part 1 and Part 2 are **not** a second registration track; they **live on gold Stage 1**.

**Employment** is a **project loop** — feasibility through contract administration — and a **separate hours line** into Stage 2. Project phases are not the same as logged hours.

**Legislation** is an **instrument**: write, interpret, amend. The NCC, the Act, the code of conduct sit here as documents that can be rewritten.

**Ethics** is a cycle: **knowledge, power, public, knowledge**. Lui’s point: a code of conduct can be rewritten; that rewrite is a power move, not only a moral text.

**Bodies** **split**. The **Institute** is voluntary — advocacy, awards, CPDs. The **ARBV** is statutory — register, prosecute. Same field, different force.

**Procurement** is a **fork of three** contracts: traditional, novated design and construct, managing contractor. That fork is **independent research**, not from the set readings. It is the loose side of the matrix: who carries risk depends on which contract is marked.

**Research** is a **contest**: who may rewrite the code, who may rewrite the matrix.

**Power** is the **smaller overlapping hub** — not a second gold path.

**Critical position (close):**

Beaton treats the title as an **institution**, not a personal trait. Loos, on ornament, is a reminder that taste and class hide inside “professional” appearance. The AIA *Code of Conduct* is **voluntary**; it is not the Act. So the diagram’s claim is: **one tight path to the title**, and **looser marks** for conduct and contract — and the title on the sheet is the question that remains.

## If they ask questions (backup)

Full practise answers: `04-practise-qa.md`. One-liners:

| Question | One-sentence answer |
|---|---|
| Why stigmergy, not a flowchart? | Because groups **co-produce** marks in one field; a flowchart would fake a single owner. |
| Why is gold larger? | Registration is the **tight** statutory path; size is hierarchy of force, not “more important as a person.” |
| Fail vs ejected? | Fail **re-queues** on the dotted loop. Ejection is **Tribunal** after a serious breach. |
| Why don’t other groups have Stage 1, 2, 3? | Those words belong to **registration**. Copying them would pretend Education or Employment is a second ARBV track. |
| What are the grey words? | Relation verbs only: enables, limits, depends upon, influences, contrasts. |
| Where is TRUST / RISK from? | Tutorial **whiteboard**, not a publication. |
| Procurement — which reading? | **None of the set four.** Independent. Traditional / D&C / MC. |
| Beaton? | Title as **institution** — who is trusted to hold it. |
| Loos? | Ornament / taste / class inside what looks like professional dress. Collection and page **not confirmed** in the notes — do not invent a page number. |
| Lui? | Code of conduct as something that can be **rewritten**; rewrite = power. Year/pages **not confirmed**. |
| AIA Code? | **Voluntary** professional text, not the Registration Act. |
| POWER hub? | Overlap — who writes the instrument, who sits on the Tribunal — **not** a second registration ladder. |
| Chicago / sources? | Four set readings in the bibliography file; procurement flagged independent; TRUST/RISK flagged studio. |
