## What the task actually is

Take real SOH events and show how a contemporary architect would manage them through today's documents: **CAA 2024** (client ↔ architect) and **ABIC Simple Works 2018** (client ↔ contractor, architect administers). You are **not** reconstructing the historical contracts.

The file must work as **one coherent project record**: an event in the Timeline must be traceable through letters, agreement, contract, forms and the Document Register, with **dates, decisions, instructions and consequences consistent** everywhere.

Core relationship to demonstrate: **scope ↔ time ↔ cost ↔ quality ↔ professional obligations**.

Assumptions are allowed (the historical project didn't use these contracts) but must be **clearly distinguished from historical fact and applied consistently**.

### Part B — ARBV Practice Review Statement (all three students)

- Evidence = **specific decisions and documents from Part A** + the reasoning behind them + the **mechanisms used to maintain the integrity** of the CAA and the construction contract.
- Include **appendices** of contract variations and control documents from Part A.
- **Not** a general defence of the architect, **not** a history of the SOH.
- Anchor: *Architects Regulations 2015* (Vic) **Part 5, reg 5.2(a)(b)(c)** — act with integrity, fairness and impartiality administering the building contract; discharge administration obligations diligently and promptly; give the client relevant information about contract administration in a timely manner. `[verify exact wording against the Regulations]`
