# Chicago references (notes-bibliography)

Default for this repo: **Chicago Manual of Style — notes and bibliography**.

When asked to “make a reference list,” produce a **Bibliography** (alphabetical by author surname) unless footnotes/endnotes are requested.

---

## Bibliography examples

### Book

Lastname, Firstname. *Title of Book*. Place: Publisher, Year.

Smith, Jane. *Professional Practice in Context*. Melbourne: Example Press, 2020.

### Chapter in edited book

Lastname, Firstname. “Chapter Title.” In *Book Title*, edited by Editor Name, 12–34. Place: Publisher, Year.

### Journal article

Lastname, Firstname. “Article Title.” *Journal Name* 12, no. 3 (2021): 45–67. https://doi.org/xx.xxxx/xxxxx.

### Webpage / online report

Lastname, Firstname (or Organisation). “Page Title.” Website Name. Month Day, Year. URL.

World Health Organization. “Health Topics.” Accessed March 1, 2025. https://www.who.int/health-topics.

### Lecture / LMS material (if allowed by your unit)

Instructor Lastname, Firstname. “Lecture Title.” Lecture, Course Code, Institution, Month Day, Year.

---

## Footnote / endnote examples (first citation)

1. Jane Smith, *Professional Practice in Context* (Melbourne: Example Press, 2020), 42.

2. Jane Smith, “Article Title,” *Journal Name* 12, no. 3 (2021): 50, https://doi.org/xx.xxxx/xxxxx.

### Shortened subsequent note

3. Smith, *Professional Practice*, 55.
